| **D-04** | **Baseline Model & Candidate Promotion** | Lock **Logistic Regression as Baseline Champion** and evaluate **LightGBM as Candidate Model** strictly on identical splits. | Baseline Logistic Regression achieved `0.1124` PR-AUC and `0.5549` ROC-AUC. | **D-05** | **Probability Calibration Method** | Apply **Sigmoid (Platt Scaling)** fitted on 5-fold `GroupKFold` out-of-fold logit predictions. | Platt scaling completely eliminated severe baseline calibration bias ($+0.4021 \to 0.0000$) and lowered Brier score to $0.0824$ (Brier Skill Score $+0.0055$ vs baseline $0.0829$), while strictly preserving rank-ordering discrimination (PR-AUC $0.1173$). | **D-06** | **Operational Risk Thresholds & Capacity Policy** | Lock in validated cutoffs **$\tau_{\text{low}} = 0.110$** and **$\tau_{\text{med}} = 0.140$** with a strict dynamic **$\le 20\%$ fleet intervention capacity ceiling**. | Sweeping asymmetric loss across 3 cost ratios proved $\tau = 0.110$ captures $21.36\%$ of all failures with $13.44\%$ precision and reduces net failure costs by $7.6\%$ under Scenario 2, while staying strictly within vehicle capacity ($14.66\% \le 20.0\%$). `RiskEngine.apply_capacity_constrained_policy()` enforces a Top-$K$ ranking fallback if volume exceeds fleet capacity. | Arbitrary cutoffs ($0.10 / 0.20$); unconstrained thresholding without capacity ceiling; pure static quantile ranking. | Validated in `docs/THRESHOLD_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` and wired into `RiskEngine` risk tiers. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.3)** |
| **D-07** | **Unified Explainability Engine in Calibrated Log-Odds Space** | Implement **Linear SHAP (`LinearExplainerChampion`)** for Champion Logistic Regression and **TreeSHAP (`TreeExplainerCandidate`)** for Candidate LightGBM operating strictly in the **calibrated log-odds margin space** with dual-compatible schema. | Closed-form Linear SHAP achieves 0.091ms attribution latency with exact machine-precision local accuracy ($\sum \phi_j \equiv z_{\text{cal}} - \text{base\_value}$). Precomputing 73-dimensional development feature expectations eliminates runtime sampling. Platt calibration affine scaling ($z \mapsto A z + B$) scales attributions uniformly while preserving rank ordering. Dual-compatible schema preserves legacy keys (`factor`, `detail`, `severity`) alongside signed $\Delta \text{logit}$ attributions and bounded `risk_driver_share`. | Explaining uncalibrated probabilities via heuristic approximations; KernelSHAP; silent fallback to legacy heuristics. | Fully wired into `RiskEngine.predict_risk()` and `predict_batch()`. Verified by 12 new unit tests (56/56 total regression tests passing). | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.5)** |
