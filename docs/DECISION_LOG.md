| **D-01** | **Authoritative Feature Schema** | Lock in **44-Feature Contract** (4 categorical + 40 numerical) with 6 interaction features and Laplace-smoothed customer propensity. | Verified in `src/feature_engineering.py`. | **D-02** | **Target Formulation & Baseline Imbalance** | Use latent-logit synthetic target `first_attempt_failed` with **~9.14% failure prevalence**. | Raw Amazon `scan_status` has an extreme 0.76% failure rate causing severe classifier degeneracy. Real-world urban last-mile carriers (DHL/UPS) experience 8–12% failure rates. | Raw 0.76% Amazon `scan_status`; SMOTE oversampling. | Model performance evaluates synthetic latent relationships. Documentation must honestly disclose synthetic target boundaries. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED** |
| **D-03** | **Validation & Split Strategy** | Split dataset using **`GroupShuffleSplit` grouped strictly by `route_id`** (Train 70%, Val 15%, Test 15%). | Parcels on the same route share vehicle space, driver pacing, and traffic. Random splitting causes massive spatial-temporal data leakage. | Random `train_test_split`; purely temporal split. | Preprocessors and imputers must be fitted **exclusively on the Train split**. Zero route contamination between Train and Test. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED** |
| **D-04** | **Baseline Model & Candidate Promotion** | Lock **Logistic Regression as Baseline Champion** and evaluate **LightGBM as Candidate Model** strictly on identical splits. | Baseline Logistic Regression achieved `0.1124` PR-AUC and `0.5549` ROC-AUC. Candidate LightGBM achieved `0.1174` PR-AUC (+4.4% relative) and `0.5623` ROC-AUC (+1.3% relative). However, because candidate remained below the mandatory promotion gate (PR-AUC $\ge 0.1500$, ROC-AUC $\ge 0.6500$), baseline Logistic Regression was preserved as Champion under strict governance. | Automatically promoting candidate without meeting threshold gates; deep tabular models. | Baseline artifacts preserved in `models/champion_model.joblib`. Candidate serialized in `models/lightgbm_candidate.joblib`. Both switchable in `RiskEngine`. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Gate Enforced)** |
| **D-05** | **Probability Calibration Method** | Apply **Sigmoid (Platt Scaling)** fitted on 5-fold `GroupKFold` out-of-fold logit predictions. | Platt scaling completely eliminated severe baseline calibration bias ($+0.4021 \to 0.0000$) and lowered Brier score to $0.0824$ (Brier Skill Score $+0.0055$ vs baseline $0.0829$), while strictly preserving rank-ordering discrimination (PR-AUC $0.1173$). Isotonic regression was evaluated and rejected because its piecewise step function introduced flat-step plateaus that degraded PR-AUC to $0.1157$. | Isotonic regression; raw uncalibrated probabilities; temperature scaling. | Calibrators serialized in `models/calibrator_champion.joblib` and `models/calibrator_candidate.joblib`. `RiskEngine` automatically calibrates raw model probabilities during inference. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.3)** |
| **D-06** | **Operational Risk Thresholds & Capacity Policy** | Lock in validated cutoffs **$\tau_{\text{low}} = 0.110$** and **$\tau_{\text{med}} = 0.140$** with a strict dynamic **$\le 20\%$ fleet intervention capacity ceiling**. | Sweeping asymmetric loss across 3 cost ratios proved $\tau = 0.110$ captures $21.36\%$ of all failures with $13.44\%$ precision and reduces net failure costs by $7.6\%$ under Scenario 2, while staying strictly within vehicle capacity ($14.66\% \le 20.0\%$). `RiskEngine.apply_capacity_constrained_policy()` enforces a Top-$K$ ranking fallback if volume exceeds fleet capacity. | Arbitrary cutoffs ($0.10 / 0.20$); unconstrained thresholding without capacity ceiling; pure static quantile ranking. | Validated in `docs/THRESHOLD_VALIDATION_REPORT.md` and wired into `RiskEngine` risk tiers. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.3)** |
| **D-07** | **Model Explainability Engine** | Replace rule-based heuristics with **TreeSHAP (`shap.TreeExplainer`)** to extract Top-3 positive risk drivers in $< 10\text{ ms}$. | Current `_extract_risk_factors()` uses hardcoded if/else rules that guess reasons independently of actual tree splits. TreeSHAP provides exact mathematical Shapley attributions. | KernelSHAP (exponentially slow); LLM-based narrative generation (slow, non-deterministic). | Requires user authorization to install `shap` package. Feature names must match ColumnTransformer output indices exactly. Non-causal mathematical reporting enforced. | **RECOMMENDED (Awaiting Phase 3.5 Approval)** |
| **D-08** | **Analytical Data Layer & Benchmarks** | Integrate **DuckDB** for analytical aggregations, station rollups, and SQL-native zero-leakage filtering, while preserving Pandas for inference. | Measured benchmarks prove DuckDB is **1.80x faster for multi-station analytical aggregations** (9.95ms vs 17.93ms) with 0 feature mismatches across 100k rows. Direct raw CSV reads are 0.78x due to DataFrame conversion overhead. | Polars; pure Pandas `read_csv`; SQLite in-memory. | DuckDB used for monitoring, batch querying, and Parquet scaling; Pandas retained for single-row inference. 44-feature contract strictly preserved. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.1)** |
| **D-09** | **Continual Feedback & Learning** | Implement **Conjugate Beta-Binomial Zone Prior Updating** & customer track record increments via `POST /api/delivery/complete`. | Avoids catastrophic forgetting associated with online gradient backpropagation on decision trees. Allows instantaneous zero-retrain risk adaptation. | Daily streaming tree refitting; pure static batch retraining. | Completed delivery outcomes update customer profiles and zone priors in real time. Model weights remain stable until scheduled batch refits. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED** |
| **D-10** | **Frontend Serving Architecture** | Retain and enhance **`static/index.html`** served directly by FastAPI. | The existing static dashboard is fully functional, styled, connected to all API endpoints, and requires zero Node.js build pipeline during deployment. | Rewriting dashboard in unconfigured `frontend/` Vite React app. | Saves engineering time for core ML, routing, and explainability tasks. React frontend can be treated as an optional future enhancement. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED** |
| **D-11** | **Test Database Isolation Fixture** | Introduce a **Pytest Database Isolation Fixture** in `conftest.py` using an in-memory SQLite database (`:memory:`) during test execution. | Running `pytest tests/test_api.py` currently mutates the tracked file `data/bridge_local.db` due to server startup seeding and delivery outcome writes, creating uncommitted Git dirty states. | Running tests on tracked database; committing mutated binary files. | Decouples automated testing from tracked repository files. Guarantees `git status` remains 100% clean after test runs. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.1)** |
| **D-12** | **LightGBM Candidate Architecture & Promotion Gate Enforcement** | Retain **Logistic Regression as Champion** while serializing **LightGBM as Candidate Model** (`models/lightgbm_candidate.joblib`). | LightGBM achieved PR-AUC `0.1174` (vs baseline `0.1124`) and ROC-AUC `0.5623` (vs baseline `0.5549`) with Brier score `0.0823` and latency `0.0061 ms/sample`. While achieving superior discrimination over baseline, it did not satisfy the mandatory minimum promotion target (PR-AUC $\ge 0.1500$, ROC-AUC $\ge 0.6500$). Governance rules strictly prohibit unearned promotion. | Prematurely declaring LightGBM champion; discarding candidate model. | Both models preserved. `RiskEngine` supports zero-downtime hot-swapping. Metadata explicitly audits promotion gate status. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.2)** |
| **D-13** | **Quarantined Test Protocol & Transfer Validity Audit** | Isolate final evaluation on an untouched **Quarantined Test Set (`seed=2026`, N=14,819)** and audit calibration transfer via **Kolmogorov-Smirnov test**. | Test labels must never influence calibrator fitting or threshold sweeps. KS tests ($D=0.0105$ for LR, $D=0.0140$ for LGB, both $D \ll 0.05$) mathematically verified that OOF calibration functions transfer cleanly to full-development base models without distributional distortion. Historical continuity partition (`seed=42`) audited separately. | Evaluating and tuning on the same test set; assuming identical score distributions without empirical audit. | Zero test-set leakage guaranteed. All promotion decisions, thresholds, and calibrators proven robust across distinct route partitions. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.3)** |
| **D-14** | **Risk Banding Centralization & Capacity Separation** | Centralize 3-tier risk banding in **`RiskEngine._assign_risk_band`**, add **`predict_batch`**, strictly decouple statistical risk bands from operational capacity constraints in **`apply_capacity_constrained_policy`**, and enforce deterministic tie-breaking. | Risk tiers (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`) are mathematical functions of calibrated probability and must never be downgraded when fleet capacity is exceeded. Capacity status (`APPROVED`, `DEFERRED_DUE_TO_FLEET_CAPACITY`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE_LOW_RISK`) handles physical constraints independently. Sorting by `(-failure_probability, input_idx)` guarantees reproducible tie-breaking while output preserves input ordering. | Modifying risk tiers based on capacity constraints; non-deterministic sorting; duplicate ad-hoc banding in multiple endpoints. | Single and batch inference pathways are 100% consistent; API consumers receive stable ordering and clear separation between risk metrics and operational dispatch decisions. | **APPROVED & VERIFIED (Phase 3.4)** |
