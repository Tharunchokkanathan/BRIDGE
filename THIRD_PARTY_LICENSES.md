## BRIDGE Synthetic Data & Authored Code Notice

The BRIDGE project develops independent methodologies on top of baseline delivery structures:
- **Synthetic Features & Target:** All customer profile features (`customer_availability_rate`, `customer_previous_delivery_attempts`, `customer_previous_failed_attempts`, `customer_previous_success_rate`, `customer_delivery_preference`, `preferred_delivery_method`, `signature_required`), premises attributes (`address_access_difficulty`), environmental risk forecasts (`traffic_risk_score`, `weather_risk_score`), historical priors, and the `first_attempt_failed` target variable are **synthetically generated by the BRIDGE project** and do not originate from Amazon.
